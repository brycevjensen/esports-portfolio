Esports 101

Rocket League

"Soccer with rocket-powered cars." Two teams of three fly across the arena trying to hit a massive ball into the opposing net. It's fast-paced, highly mechanical, and entirely physics-based. A true crowd favorite.

Valorant

A 5v5 tactical shooter where players choose "Agents" with unique abilities. One team tries to plant a bomb (the Spike), while the other defends. Precision aim, economy management, and strategic ability usage are key.

Overwatch 2

A vibrant 5v5 hero shooter requiring intense teamwork. Players select heroes from Tank, Damage, or Support categories to capture objectives or escort payloads across the map. Ultimate economy is the name of the game.

League of Legends

A 5v5 MOBA (Multiplayer Online Battle Arena) where teams race to destroy the opposing team's base (Nexus). It requires deep strategic knowledge, objective control, map awareness, and intense mechanical skill.
